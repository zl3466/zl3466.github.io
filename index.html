About Me

I am currently an Applied Scientist Intern on the Navigation team at Amazon Frontier AI & Robotics (FAR), working with Prof. Chen Feng. I received my M.S. in ECE from Carnegie Mellon University, where I worked on vision-language robot perception at the AirLab, and my B.S. in ECE with a CS minor from NYU Tandon, where I worked with Prof. Chen Feng at the AI4CE Lab and with Dr. Shuo Chen on neuroscience. My current research focuses on robot perception, mapping, and navigation.
